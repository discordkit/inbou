Add `/goi start`, a JLPT N5 vocabulary mock test shaped like the paper's Section 1: 7 kanji to read, 5 words to write, 6 blanks to fill and 3 sentences to match, in 20 minutes. It ships with 490 hand-written, reviewed questions, each checked against JMdict so that no wrong option is really a reading or spelling of the word, and no question in a test gives another's answer away. Students who choose a wrong option on a medium or hard question are told privately why. My results now continues in further private messages when a student's explanations do not fit in one. The privacy policy now notes that a vocabulary test holds the same short-lived reply tokens as a reading test
